## Presentation

AVIF/WebP derivatives are produced with `scripts/art-assets.mjs`, with `withoutEnlargement: true`. They preserve the composition and do not add invented detail, remove watermarks, recolour the original files, or incorporate the artist’s images as Endi’s own authored artwork. Decorative previews and the footer shader transform the displayed surface only. The 2000px Sand Spline file supplies the expanding hero; the 800px piece remains limited to its native width in the case study. All pages link to source files, original downloads and the licences.

Museum and Openverse APIs returned HTTP 403 in this environment; no art was obtained from those services. No museum provenance is claimed.
